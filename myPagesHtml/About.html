ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Nfansu O Barrow, a dedicated Embedded Systems and AI engineering student currently pursuing my Master's degree at ENSAF (École Nationale des Sciences Appliquées de Fès).

As a first-year Master's student specializing in Embedded Systems and AI Engineering, I am passionate about designing intelligent systems that seamlessly integrate hardware and software. My primary areas of focus include embedded systems development, AI-driven applications, computer vision, machine learning, and real-time computing, with practical experience in microcontroller programming and deployment.

Recently, I have been actively engaged in diverse projects spanning artificial intelligence, computer vision, web development, and microcontroller-based systems. These experiences have strengthened my technical foundation and expanded my problem-solving capabilities across multiple domains.

Currently, I have completed an internship at CHU (Centre Hospitalier Universitaire), where I have developed a Vehicle Management System for hospital parking operations. This project leverages computer vision technology to optimize parking management and enhance operational efficiency.

Beyond my technical pursuits, I value collaboration and knowledge sharing within the engineering community. I believe that engineering is not only about building innovative solutions but also about continuous learning, personal growth, and creating meaningful impact. In my free time, I enjoy watching football and maintaining an active lifestyle through regular training.

This portfolio serves as a showcase of my projects, technical skills, and professional experiences as I advance in my career as an embedded systems engineer. I invite you to explore my work and follow my journey as I continue to develop innovative solutions at the intersection of embedded systems and artificial intelligence.
